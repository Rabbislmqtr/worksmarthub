How to Calculate Profit Margin

Profit margin shows how much of your revenue remains after a cost is deducted. It is a useful starting point for checking whether a price covers the work required to sell a product or service.

The formula

Profit margin = (Revenue − Cost) ÷ Revenue × 100

For example, if a sale brings in $100 and the associated cost is $60, gross profit is $40. The margin is $40 ÷ $100 × 100 = 40%.

Margin is not markup

Margin divides profit by revenue. Markup divides profit by cost. The same sale can therefore have different margin and markup percentages. Mixing the two is a common pricing mistake.

What to include in your thinking

  • Separate direct costs from overhead.
  • Check whether the calculation is per sale, month, or year.
  • Account for payment fees, returns, and discounts where relevant.

Limitations

This simple calculation is educational and does not replace a complete set of accounts. For important decisions, confirm the numbers with your accounting records or a qualified professional.
