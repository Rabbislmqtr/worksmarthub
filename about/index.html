About the project

Useful tools before advertising

WorkSmart Hub is being built to help freelancers and small businesses make clearer everyday decisions without requiring an account or expensive software.

Our purpose

Many business questions are simple but time-consuming: what is my margin, what price covers my costs, or how many sales do I need to break even? We turn those questions into understandable tools and explanations.

What we do not do

  • We do not promise income, rankings, or business results.
  • We do not provide personalized tax, legal, medical, or investment advice.
  • We do not publish fake reviews, testimonials, or statistics.
  • We do not encourage visitors to click advertisements.

How the site may be supported

Advertising may be introduced after the site has sufficient original content and a real user audience. Ads will remain clearly separated from tools and editorial content.
