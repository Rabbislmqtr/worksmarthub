How to Calculate Your Break-Even Point

The break-even point is the sales level where revenue covers the selected costs. It is a planning estimate, not a prediction of demand.

Units formula

Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ (Price per unit − Variable cost per unit)

With $2,000 fixed costs, a $50 price, and a $30 variable cost, break-even is 2,000 ÷ 20 = 100 units.

Use consistent periods

If fixed costs are monthly, your sales and variable costs should also be monthly. Include only costs that belong to the same decision.

Where the estimate can fail

Demand, discounts, inventory constraints, changing costs, and multiple products can make a simple single-product model less realistic. Use it as a conversation starter and update it with actual data.
