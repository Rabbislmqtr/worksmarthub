How to Calculate Return on Investment

ROI compares the net return from an investment with the amount invested. It can help organize a decision, but it depends on how the return is measured.

ROI = (Return − Investment cost) ÷ Investment cost × 100

For a $500 campaign that produces $750 in attributable return, net return is $250 and ROI is 50%.

Use the ROI calculator

Define return carefully

Revenue is not always profit. If a campaign produces sales, subtract the relevant product costs and fees when evaluating business return.

Consider timing

Two investments can have the same ROI but take different amounts of time. Add a time period and compare the quality and reliability of the result.
