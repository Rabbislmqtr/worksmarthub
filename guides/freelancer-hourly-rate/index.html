How Much Should a Freelancer Charge Per Hour?

An hourly rate is a planning number. It should reflect the income you need and the portion of your working time that can actually be billed.

A simple starting formula

Hourly rate = Target annual income ÷ Billable hours per year

If your goal is $60,000 and you expect 1,200 billable hours, the starting rate is $50 per hour.

Estimate your hourly rate

Billable hours are not total hours

Sales, proposals, admin, learning, leave, and business development take time but may not be invoiced. Overestimating billable hours makes a rate look safer than it is.

Review the package

Compare hourly pricing with project or retainer pricing where the scope is clear. Explain deliverables and revision limits rather than relying only on a number.
