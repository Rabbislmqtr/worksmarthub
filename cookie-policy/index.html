Cookie Policy

This page explains whether WorkSmart Hub uses cookies and what that means for you. The short version: this site does not set its own cookies, and it does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Last updated: 2026-09-21

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website can ask your browser to store so that it can recognise your device on a later visit. Related technologies, such as browser storage, can hold information in a similar way.

What WorkSmart Hub actually does

WorkSmart Hub sets no cookies of its own. There is no login and no saved preferences, so there is nothing about you for the site to remember. Your calculator figures are used in memory while the page is open and disappear when you reload or leave. You can confirm this yourself by opening a calculator, disconnecting from the internet, and continuing to use it.

Strictly necessary security

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may apply strictly necessary security measures when serving the site, which can include a short-lived technical cookie used to tell ordinary visitors apart from automated traffic. These measures exist to keep the site available, and they are not used to build a profile of you or for marketing. Blocking them may affect whether the site loads.

If we introduce analytics or advertising

Advertising is not currently enabled. If the site is accepted into an advertising programme such as Google AdSense, and if analytics is added to understand which tools are useful, this page will be updated first to name each provider and to describe the purpose and duration of what it stores. Where the law requires it — including for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — non-essential cookies will load only after you give consent, and you will be able to change or withdraw that choice. Declining will never block the calculators.

When advertising is enabled, you will also be able to control personalised advertising through your Google Ads settings, and you can limit or delete cookies in your browser at any time.

Managing cookies in your browser

Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies, and browse in a private window. Blocking cookies will not stop the WorkSmart Hub calculators from working, because they do not depend on stored data.
