How to Track Business Expenses

Consistent expense records help you understand where money goes and give your accountant better information.

Record expenses close to the purchase

Capture the date, amount, supplier, category, payment method, and a short business purpose. Keep the receipt according to your local record-keeping requirements.

Choose useful categories

Start with categories that help decisions: tools, materials, marketing, travel, contractors, fees, and office costs. Avoid creating so many categories that recording becomes a burden.

Track an expense

Review trends, not just totals

Look for recurring subscriptions, rising fees, and costs that do not support current work. For tax treatment, follow local guidance or ask a qualified professional.
