How to Create a Simple Small-Business Budget

A budget is a forward-looking plan that lets you compare what you expect with what actually happened.

Use four sections

  1. Expected income by product or service.
  2. Fixed costs such as subscriptions or rent.
  3. Flexible costs such as materials and marketing.
  4. Cash reserve or planned savings.

Keep assumptions visible

Write down whether each income figure is a target, confirmed order, or conservative estimate. Separate one-time costs from recurring costs so one unusual month does not distort your view.

Review monthly

Compare the budget with actual records, explain the biggest differences, and update the next month. A simple budget used consistently is more useful than a detailed sheet nobody reviews.
