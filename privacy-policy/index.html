Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to information when you use WorkSmart Hub. The short version: every calculator runs inside your own browser, there are no accounts, and no analytics or advertising technology is loaded on this site.

Last updated: 2026-09-21

What we do not collect

You do not need an account, a name, or an email address to use WorkSmart Hub. We do not ask for personal information in order to use the tools, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

Your calculator input stays on your device

The calculators are ordinary page scripts. The figures you type — revenue, costs, rates, prices — are calculated by your own browser and are never sent to us or to anyone else. Nothing is saved when you close or reload the page, and no history of your calculations is kept.

You can verify this yourself: open any calculator, disconnect from the internet, and keep using it. If results depended on a server, they would stop working.

Technical data our hosting provider processes

Like every website, requests to WorkSmart Hub are handled by a hosting provider (Cloudflare). To deliver pages and defend against abuse and automated attacks, that provider processes routine request data such as IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. We do not use this data to identify individual visitors, and we do not combine it with any other information.

Cookies

WorkSmart Hub does not set its own cookies and does not use browser storage to keep information about you. There is no login, so there is nothing for the site to remember. The Cookie Policy explains this in more detail.

If you email us

The Contact page lists an email address. If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem you reported. Messages are held in a standard mailbox operated by Google (Gmail) and are kept only as long as they are useful. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell or share your address.

Analytics and advertising

There is currently no analytics script and no advertising code on this website. If that changes, this policy and the Cookie Policy will be updated before anything is switched on, the providers and their purposes will be named, and non-essential technology will load only after you consent where the law requires it. You will always be able to decline without losing access to the tools.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal information, such as asking what is held about you, asking for it to be corrected, or asking for it to be deleted. In practice we hold almost nothing about visitors. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete that correspondence and we will do so.

Children

WorkSmart Hub is intended for adults running or planning a business. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change.
