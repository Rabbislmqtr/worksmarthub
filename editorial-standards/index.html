Editorial standards

How we review our tools and guides

A useful website is built through careful review, not by publishing the most pages.

Before publishing

  • Check the formula and edge cases.
  • Explain assumptions and limitations.
  • Use original examples and clear language.
  • Check internal links, metadata, headings, and mobile layout.
  • Review claims against reliable primary sources when facts may change.

AI use

AI may assist with organizing research or suggesting an outline, but it is not an automatic publishing system. Published material must be reviewed for accuracy, usefulness, originality, and clarity.

Updates and corrections

We update pages when formulas, assumptions, or relevant information changes. Readers can report issues through the Contact page.
