Profit Margin vs. Markup: What’s the Difference?

Both percentages describe profit, but they use different denominators. Knowing which one you are using prevents confusing pricing conversations.

Markup

Markup = (Selling price − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100

If cost is $60 and price is $100, markup is 66.67%.

Margin

Margin = (Selling price − Cost) ÷ Selling price × 100

With the same numbers, margin is 40%. Neither percentage is wrong; they answer different questions.

A practical habit

Write the word “margin” or “markup” beside the percentage in your price sheet. Avoid presenting an unlabeled percentage to customers or teammates.
